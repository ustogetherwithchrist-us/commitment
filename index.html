A Prayer for Us

Heavenly Father, thank You for bringing us together and for every step of our story.

Be the center of our love, the third strand of our cord. Teach us to love as Christ loves, to serve as He served, and to forgive as we have been forgiven.

Keep our hearts pure and faithful while we wait for the day You have prepared. Bless our families, guide our steps, and make our home a place where Your name is honored.

Prepare us for the children You may give us, and help us raise them to know and love You.

Until the day we say “I do,” and every day after, draw us closer to You and closer to each other.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.
